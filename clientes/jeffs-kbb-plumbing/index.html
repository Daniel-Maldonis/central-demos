Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common questions regarding our plumbing services.

How much does a plumbing service call typically cost? +
At Jeff’s KB&B Plumbing, we offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Our standard service call fee covers the initial inspection and diagnosis, and is credited toward the cost of any repairs or installations you choose to proceed with. Repipe estimates are always 100% free!
How do I know if my Orlando home needs a repipe? +
Key warning signs include discolored water, sudden drops in water pressure, multiple pinhole leaks, high monthly water bills, or an older home constructed with galvanized or polybutylene pipes. Our licensed plumbers inspect your system and provide an honest assessment.
What brands and types of water heaters do you install? +
We install traditional tank and energy-efficient tankless water heaters from industry-leading brands, including Bradford White. We also offer 5% off water heater installations for new customers!

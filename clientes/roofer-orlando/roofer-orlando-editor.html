Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about roof replacements, insurance, and costs in Central Florida.

How much does a typical roof replacement cost in Orlando?
Roof replacements in Central Florida typically range from $7,000 to $16,000+ for asphalt shingles depending on square footage, pitch, and code requirements. Tile and standing seam metal roofs sit higher due to their 40-50+ year lifespans. We provide free on-site inspections with exact, written pricing.
Are you licensed and insured in the State of Florida?
Yes. We hold an active Certified Roofing Contractor license (CCC1333142) issued by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR). We also carry $2,000,000 in general liability and complete workers' compensation insurance.
How long does a roof replacement take?
Most single-family residential shingle replacements in the Orlando area are finished in 1 to 2 days, weather permitting. Tile and complex metal projects typically take 3 to 5 days.
Will my homeowner's insurance cover roof damage?
If your roof has sustained damage from storms, hail, fallen branches, or high winds, your homeowner's insurance policy often covers the replacement cost minus your deductible. We provide complete photo documentation and damage reports to assist your adjuster.

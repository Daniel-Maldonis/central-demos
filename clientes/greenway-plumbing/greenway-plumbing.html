Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to common plumbing questions from our master plumbing staff.

How do I know if my Orlando home needs a whole-house repipe? +
Common signs include recurring pinhole leaks, rusty or discolored water when you turn on the tap, noticeable drops in water pressure when multiple fixtures are in use, or homes with older copper or Polybutylene pipes. A whole-house PEX repipe eliminates leaks permanently and upgrades your home value.
Are your plumbers licensed and insured in the State of Florida? +
Yes, Greenway Plumbing Services Corp is a fully licensed and insured State Certified Plumbing Contractor (License # CFC 1428250). Working with a licensed contractor protects you from liability and ensures all work complies strictly with the Florida Plumbing Code.
How does your pricing work? Will there be hidden fees? +
We provide upfront, transparent pricing. Our technician evaluates your plumbing system, diagnoses the root cause, and provides a clear written estimate before any work begins. There are never surprise fees or unexpected costs at completion.
What should I do in a plumbing emergency before the technician arrives? +
First, locate your home’s main water shutoff valve (usually by the exterior water meter or near the front hose bibb) and turn it off clockwise. Next, open an outdoor faucet to relieve pressure in the system, and call our office immediately at (407) 234-0003 for emergency dispatch.
